Questions agents ask

Which printers do you work with?

We're choosing them now. At launch you'll pick yours from a list when you order, and we'll only list printers we can upload to.

What if my listing isn't live yet?

Upload your photos, a PDF or screenshots of the draft listing, and a floor plan if you have one. We build from those.

Can I change the brochure after it's done?

Yes. On your proof, click the photo or line you want changed and type what you want in plain English.

Do you redraw floor plans?

Yes. Send the plan you have and we redraw it to match your design.

Does the listing price go on the pieces?

No. If the price changes, you'd have to reprint every flyer. To ask for an exception, or for a personalized design, email support@brochuredesk.com.
